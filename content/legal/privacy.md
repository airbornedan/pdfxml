# Privacy

_Last updated: 2026-08-29._

This describes what happens to data when you use this instance of
PDFXML. It reflects how the software actually works; if you run your
own instance, adjust it to match your setup.

## Not available in the EU / EEA / UK

This service is not offered to people in the European Union, the
European Economic Area, or the United Kingdom, and is not intended to
process the personal data of anyone located there. Do not use it from
those regions.

## What is collected

- **Files you upload.** The PDF you upload is written to temporary
  storage on the server so it can be processed.
- **A session cookie.** One signed cookie holds a random token for your
  current upload and your place in the extraction wizard. It is not an
  identifier, is not shared, and carries no tracking data.
- **Server logs.** The web server records the requesting IP address,
  the request path, and a timestamp. These are used only for
  debugging and for detecting and blocking abuse, and are rotated on
  the host's normal schedule.

There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no
third-party trackers.

## How uploaded files are handled

- Each PDF is parsed and rendered in an isolated, resource-limited
  subprocess. It is not opened by a person, indexed, or used for any
  purpose other than producing the output you asked for.
- Uploaded PDFs and their generated output (XML fragments, PNGs) are
  deleted automatically after about 20 minutes of inactivity, when you
  start a new upload, or when the server restarts -- whichever comes
  first.
- Nothing about your file is sent to any third party.

## What is not done

- Files are not retained after the window above.
- Data is not sold, rented, or shared.
- No profile of you is built.

## Your choices

Because there is no account, there is nothing to log into and nothing
to delete on request -- your data is already gone shortly after you
stop using the tool. Clearing the site's cookie ends your session
immediately.
