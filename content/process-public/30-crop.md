# Crop an image

The **Crop** tool is separate from PDF extraction and runs entirely in
your browser -- nothing is uploaded.

1. Choose an image file (PNG or JPEG) -- click the box or drop a file
   onto it.
2. Drag a rectangle over the area you want to keep.
3. Choose **Crop selection**. The cropped image appears in a dialog:
   name it in the file-name field and choose **Save PNG**.
