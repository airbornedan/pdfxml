    <title xinfo:text="189219">Useful Formulas for Calculations</title>
    <para xinfo:text="189247">
      <emphasis role="bold">NH₃ Flow Rate Calculation</emphasis>
    </para>
    <para xinfo:text="189220">Calculate the hourly NH₃ requirement using the following formula:</para>
    <para xinfo:text="189221">(Lb N/ac × Speed × Width × 0.1212) ÷ 0.822 = NH₃ lb/hr</para>
    <para xinfo:text="189222">where:</para>
    <itemizedlist>
      <listitem>
        <para xinfo:text="189223">60 = minutes per hour</para>
      </listitem>
      <listitem>
        <para xinfo:text="189224">5.15 = approximate weight of NH₃ in pounds per gallon</para>
      </listitem>
    </itemizedlist>
    <para xinfo:text="189248">
      <emphasis role="bold">Gallons Per Minute (GPM)</emphasis>
    </para>
    <para xinfo:text="189226">To convert NH₃ flow from pounds per hour to gallons per minute:</para>
    <para xinfo:text="189227">NH₃ lb/hr ÷ 60 ÷ 5.15 = NH₃ GPM</para>
    <para xinfo:text="189249">where:</para>
    <itemizedlist>
      <listitem>
        <para xinfo:text="189228">60 = minutes per hour</para>
      </listitem>
      <listitem>
        <para xinfo:text="189229">5.15 = approximate weight of NH₃ in pounds per gallon</para>
      </listitem>
    </itemizedlist>
