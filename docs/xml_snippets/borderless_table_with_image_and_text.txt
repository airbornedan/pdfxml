            <orderedlist>

                <para xinfo:text="184952">Install the ECU on your implement or tractor in a location where it will not interfere with operation. Use the provided bracket and bolts to mount the ECU. The bracket supports both vertical and horizontal mounting of the ECU. <emphasis role="bold">Note:</emphasis> When mounted vertically, the connector should point downward.<indexterm><primary>ECU</primary><secondary>orientation</secondary></indexterm></para>
              </listitem>
              <listitem>
                <para xinfo:text="184954">If necessary, route the provided ISO extension cable from the implement hitch to the desired Sentinel ECU mounting location.</para>
              </listitem>
              <listitem>
                <para xinfo:text="184955">Locate the 2-pin Molex power connector and the 12-pin Ampseal POWER/CAN connector or the 9-pin ISO connector that the Sentinel ECU will be connected to. These connectors are labeled. Remove the terminator.</para>
              </listitem>
              <listitem>
                <para xinfo:text="184956">Connect the harness connector labeled ECU to the Sentinel ECU.</para>
              </listitem>
              <listitem>
                <para xinfo:text="184957">Connect the other end of the harness to the Molex/Ampseal POWER/CAN connection.</para>
              </listitem>
              <listitem>
                <para xinfo:text="184958">Use the previously removed terminator to terminate the ISO OUT POWER/CAN connection found on the ECU harness.</para>
